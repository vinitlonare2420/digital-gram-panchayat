Frequently asked questions

Do I need to visit the Panchayat office at all?

Most steps, from application to tracking, can be completed online. You may still need to visit the office to collect physical documents in some cases.

What if the office needs more documents from me?

Staff can ask for a missing or replacement document. The request, with their message, appears at the top of that application in My Applications, where you can upload the documents it asks for.

What file formats can I upload for supporting documents?

PDF, JPG, JPEG and PNG files are accepted, each up to 5 MB in size.

Is this the official Gram Panchayat website?

No. This portal is an academic prototype built to demonstrate a digital citizen service system, and is not an officially authorised government website.

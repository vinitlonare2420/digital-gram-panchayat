About This Portal

What this project is, who it is for, and what it is not.

Purpose

The Digital Gram Panchayat Citizen Service Portal is being built to let residents apply for common administrative certificates — birth, death, income and caste — online, upload the required supporting documents, and track each application through verification, review and approval, instead of making repeated trips to the Panchayat office.

Who it is for

Citizens

Apply for certificates, upload documents, track application status, respond to correction requests, and download approved certificates.

Panchayat staff

Review submitted applications, verify documents, request corrections, approve or reject applications, and upload issued certificates.

Project status

This is the frontend foundation of the portal, built as a college project. It establishes the pages, forms, navigation and interface behaviour. Authentication, application storage, document storage and staff processing will be connected to Firebase in later development phases.

A note on authenticity

This website is an academic prototype developed for evaluation purposes. It is not an officially authorised government website, and certificates issued through it (once the system is complete) are illustrative of the workflow, not legal documents, unless deployed and adopted by an actual Gram Panchayat.

Certificate Services

Select a certificate or service to view its requirements and start your application.

Applications are reviewed by Gram Panchayat staff. Some certificates are issued by other departments or authorities, so staff may process your request, reject it with a reason, or redirect you to the right office. Submitting an application does not guarantee that a certificate will be issued.

Birth Certificate

Official record of birth registration, commonly required for school admission, passports and identity documents.

Documents required

  • Hospital birth record / discharge summary
  • Parents' identity proof
  • Address proof

Death Certificate

Official record of death registration, required for insurance claims, property transfer and legal settlements.

Documents required

  • Hospital / medical death record
  • Applicant's identity proof
  • Address proof of the deceased

Income Certificate

Proof of annual family income, used for scholarships, subsidies and other government schemes.

Documents required

  • Salary slips / income proof
  • Identity proof
  • Address proof

Caste Certificate

Community/caste verification used for reservations, admissions and government benefits.

Documents required

  • Existing caste/community proof (parent's certificate, if any)
  • Identity proof
  • Address proof

Marriage Registration

Apply to have a marriage recorded with the registering authority. Details are verified against your documents before anything is registered.

Documents required

  • Proof of marriage (invitation card, photograph or similar)
  • Age proof of both spouses
  • Address proof

BPL Certificate

Request verification of Below Poverty Line status for welfare schemes, scholarships or housing benefits. Eligibility is decided by the competent authority.

Documents required

  • Identity proof of the head of family
  • Address proof
  • Income proof or ration card, if available
Apply Requirements

No Dues Certificate

Request confirmation that no house tax, water charges or other local dues are pending on a property or connection, based on the Panchayat's records.

Documents required

  • Latest property tax or water receipt
  • Identity proof
  • Ownership proof or connection details
Apply Requirements

Property Assessment Certificate

Request an assessment certificate for a property in the Panchayat area, used for tax records, transfers and loans.

Documents required

  • Ownership proof (sale deed, mutation record or tax receipt)
  • Identity proof
  • Latest property tax receipt, if any
